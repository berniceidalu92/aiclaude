# WILD KARAKORAM — Visual Analysis (State 7)

A full year in the life of an ibex family, from spring to spring. It has the same Nature's Coliseum documentary look as before, but it changes with the seasons, and the main subject is the family's everyday life rather than fights.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** photorealistic landmark wildlife documentary, 16:9 widescreen. It should look like real footage filmed by a natural-history crew from hidden positions, never CGI or a staged render.
- **Palette:** warm granite and slate greys, dusty ochre slopes, the green of alpine grass in summer, blue-white snow in winter, and huge glaciers in the background. Warm accents come from the ibex's sandy coats, the gold of low sun on rock, and the snow leopard's smoky fur. The grade is natural and gently warm, without oversaturation.
- **Lighting:** low sun at dawn and late afternoon, with rim light on fur and horns. The mountain air is very clear, with haze only over great distances.
- **Camera:** long telephoto (300–800mm) for long-range and mid-range shots, and close-range remote cameras for faces, hooves, birth and contact moments. Video uses a steady tripod or gimbal, gentle focus pulls, and true slow motion for leaps and chases.
- **Composition:** the ibex small against huge vertical walls in wide shots, to show scale and height. Ledge shots always show the drop, and ground shots show how far the cliffs are.
- **Detail:** ultra-fine detail on fur, wool, horn rings and ridges, the hoof's hard rim and soft pad, frost, snow crystals and rock grain.
- **Mood:** awe and intimacy, a life on the edge. Tense during the leopard hunts, tender at the birth and in the nursery, and never gory.

## Shot range (mix it to suit the moment)
- **Long range:** the mountain, the seasons, the herd moving, and the leopard watching or stalking from far away.
- **Mid range:** feeding, climbing, the rut displays, and chases.
- **Close range:** hooves gripping rock, the lip-curl, horn clashes, the birth, the kid's first steps, kids playing, the leopard's eyes, paws and tail, and every moment of contact.

## Seasons and lighting
- **Summer (opening, meadows, nursery):** green alpine grass, wildflowers, snow only on the high peaks, bright clear sun.
- **Autumn (the rut):** golden grass, the first snow dusting the slopes, low golden light.
- **Winter (cold, the leopard hunts):** deep snow, frozen waterfalls, steel-blue shadows, a low pale sun, breath steaming.
- **Spring (birth):** melting snow, wet rock, the first green shoots, soft morning light.
- **Times of day:**
  - dawn for the feeding trips down,
  - midday for resting on the ledges,
  - late afternoon for the salt lick,
  - night for the ending.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **Dawa:** a female Himalayan ibex with a light sandy-grey coat, short curved horns and a slim, agile build. In winter she has a thick woolly coat. Through late winter and spring she is visibly pregnant, with a rounded belly.
- **Dawa's daughter:** a young female Himalayan ibex, about one year old (later almost two), small and slim with a pale sandy-grey coat and tiny curved horns. After the leopard hunt she has a line of scratches on her back leg; by summer they have healed.
- **Kesang:** a large male Himalayan ibex with a dark brown winter coat, a dark beard, and massive curved horns over a meter long with thick knuckle-like ridges, swept back over his shoulders.
- **The old male:** a thin, stiff old male Himalayan ibex with a greying coat and very long, heavy horns with many rings.
- **The newborn kid:** a tiny Himalayan ibex kid with a soft pale-grey coat, oversized legs and no horns yet. It is wet at birth.
- **The kids:** small, playful Himalayan ibex kids with pale-grey coats and tiny horn buds.
- **The snow leopard:** an adult female snow leopard with a thick smoky-grey coat covered in black rosettes, pale green-grey eyes, huge furry paws and a long thick tail almost as long as her body.
- **The golden eagle:** a large golden eagle with dark brown feathers, a golden nape and huge yellow talons.

## Locations
1. **The cliff ledges:** sheer granite and slate walls with ledges as wide as a hand, hundreds of meters above the valley.
2. **The grazing slope:** a slope of grass and herbs at the foot of the cliffs, where the family feeds at dawn.
3. **The salt lick:** a gully at the bottom of the valley with a patch of pale, crusted earth and white mineral crust on the rocks, and boulders around it.
4. **The high meadows:** green alpine meadows above 5,000 meters, beneath glaciers.
5. **The rut slope:** an open slope of golden grass and first snow, above the herd.
6. **The winter slopes:** steep, sun-facing slopes with patches of bare grass where the snow has slid off, next to the cliffs.
7. **The flat ground:** open, flatter ground with thinner snow, a few hundred meters from the cliffs (where the old male dies).
8. **The birth ledge:** a tiny ledge on the steepest, most broken rock, barely wide enough to lie on, with a huge drop below.
9. **The nursery cliff:** a cliff with many ledges and boulders where the kids gather and play.
10. **Background:** the Karakoram in northern Pakistan, with jagged granite spires, peaks of seven to eight thousand meters, long glaciers and deep valleys.

## Continuity between beats
- Each scene keeps one season, time of day and lighting from its first beat to its last.
- Generate the first beat of each scene first, then use the previous beat's image as the reference for the next.
- Character changes carry forward: Dawa's pregnancy (late winter to the birth), the daughter's scratches (from the hunt until they heal by summer), and the kid's growth (newborn, then a playful kid, then its first trip down).

## Rules
- Every prompt stands alone, with the full character description, location, season and style every time.
- No text, logos, music, people, villages or vehicles.
- The leopard hunts are tense but never gory. The old male's death is shown with him going down in the snow and the leopard beside him, with no blood focus.
- The birth is shown tenderly and naturally: the newborn, the licking clean and the first stand, with nothing graphic.
