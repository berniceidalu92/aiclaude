## Thumbnail analysis (State 10)
- **Title:** *5 Wolves Corner a Wolverine… They Have No Idea What She's Fighting For*
- **What the thumbnail must do:** show the "5 vs 1" instantly and the wolverine refusing to back down. It must not show the kits, so the title's question ("what she's fighting for") stays open and pulls the click.
- **Proven triggers used:**
  - **Power gap:** a 12 kg animal against five wolves. The size difference must be obvious.
  - **Defiance:** she is snarling, not running. Viewers click to see how it ends.
  - **Dramatic irony:** the title tells you there's a secret, and the image doesn't reveal it.
- **Readability at phone size:**
  - one sharp subject in the centre (the wolverine), the wolves around her slightly softer,
  - a clean background (white snow and blue sky),
  - strong contrast: a dark animal on white snow.
- **Payoff in the video:** the main thumbnail is beat 1, the very first shot, so the promise is delivered in the first second.
