## Description (paste into YouTube)

Eight wolves. One old ibex. A ledge no wider than a doorway, and two hundred meters of empty air below.

In the Spiti Valley, high in the Indian Himalaya, an ibex family faces one winter where everything hunts them. Golden eagles try to knock their kid off the cliffs. A snow leopard chases its prey straight down a mountain. Deep snow drives them down into the jaws of a starving wolf pack. Their only weapon is the cliff, and the cliff can kill them too.

The Himalaya is one of the last strongholds of the snow leopard, with as few as four thousand left in the wild. But the winters are warming, the snow line is climbing, and the mountain is shrinking beneath them.
