Fifty grizzly bears. One river. And one ledge of rock that every one of them would kill for.

In Katmai, Alaska, the salmon come home every summer, and the biggest bears on Earth come with them. At the top of one waterfall is a single spot where salmon jump straight into a waiting bear's mouth. The old king, Scar, has held it for years. Now a challenger, Ridge, wants it. And caught between them is Tula, a starving mother with two newborn cubs and only a few weeks to get fat enough to survive the winter.

So why is one ledge of rock worth dying for?

🐻 In this documentary:
• Two giant male grizzlies battling for the top of the falls
• A mother bear taking on a male twice her size, twice
• A cub swept into the rapids with a male bear wading in after her
• A wolf stealing salmon from grizzlies
• Bears snorkeling, fighting underwater, and catching salmon in mid-air
