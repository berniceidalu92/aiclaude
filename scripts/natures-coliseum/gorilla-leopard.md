# A Leopard Hunts a Gorilla Family… Then the Silverback Wakes Up

Setting: the Congo Basin rainforest, central Africa. Hero: a western lowland silverback gorilla protecting his family and a two-week-old baby.
Format: every beat is a SHOT (what the camera shows, real documentary style) followed by the NARRATION line. About 12–13 minutes.
Camera key: TELE = long telephoto from a hidden position · GROUND = low remote camera at gorilla height on the forest floor · MACRO = extreme close-up · SLOW = 120 fps slow motion · DRONE = aerial · LOW-LIGHT = night camera.

---
