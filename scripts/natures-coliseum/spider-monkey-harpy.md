# Harpy Eagles Never Miss. Then They Met Her.

Setting: the Amazon rainforest, South America. Hero: a mother spider monkey with her four-month-old baby on her back. Hunters: a family of three harpy eagles (a huge female, a smaller male, and a pale-headed juvenile).
Format: every beat is a SHOT (what the camera shows, real documentary style) followed by the NARRATION line. About 11–12 minutes. Short punchline narration, a near-catch every 30–45 seconds, no calm scenes.
Lighting: natural daylight in every shot. True-to-life colours, real shadows, no colour grade, no rim light, no light beams, no glow.
Camera key: TELE = long telephoto from a hidden position · GROUND = low camera on a branch at monkey height · MACRO = extreme close-up · SLOW = 120 fps slow motion · DRONE = aerial.
