# 5 Wolves Corner a Wolverine… They Have No Idea What She’s Fighting For

Setting: the Yukon, Canada, late March. Hero: a female wolverine with two six-week-old kits hidden in a snow den.
Format: every beat is a SHOT (what the camera shows, real documentary style) followed by the NARRATION line. About 12 minutes.
Camera key: TELE = long telephoto from a hidden position · GROUND = low remote camera at wolverine height · MACRO = extreme close-up · SLOW = 120 fps slow motion · DRONE = aerial · LOW-LIGHT = night camera under the northern lights.

---

## COLD OPEN — BLOCKBUSTER (0:00–1:00)

SHOT: Deep drum hit. TELE, bright afternoon snow, clear blue sky: a wolverine stands on top of a moose carcass while five grey wolves circle her.
NARRATION: Five wolves surround a wolverine in the snow.

SHOT: SLOW, GROUND: the lead wolf lunges; she rears up snapping, and the wolf leaps back, snow spraying toward the lens.
NARRATION: The biggest one lunges. She meets it with her teeth.

SHOT: TELE: two wolves rush in from behind; she spins on the carcass to face them.
NARRATION: Two more come from behind. She spins to face them.

SHOT: MACRO: her bared teeth, frost on her dark fur, breath steaming.
NARRATION: She weighs twelve kilos. Each wolf weighs four times more.

SHOT: Hard cut on the beat. SLOW: the wolverine and a lynx rolling through deep snow, fur flying.
NARRATION: This morning, she fought a lynx for food.

SHOT: SLOW TELE: a golden eagle drops out of the sky, talons stretched out over her back.
NARRATION: At noon, an eagle came for her from the sky.

SHOT: Music drops. TELE, sunset: at the treeline, a huge grizzly rises onto its hind legs, sniffing the air.
NARRATION: And when the sun goes down, something much bigger is coming.

SHOT: DRONE: an endless white valley between mountains, one line of small tracks crossing it.
NARRATION: She has crossed thirty kilometres of snow today.

SHOT: Silence. MACRO: a hole in a deep snowdrift, two tiny pale kit faces peeking out.
NARRATION: Because behind her, two kits are waiting in the snow.

SHOT: Heartbeat sound. GROUND: back at the carcass, all five wolves step in at once.
NARRATION: She hasn't eaten in three days. Neither have they.

SHOT: SLOW: she plants her feet on the carcass and snarls back at them.
NARRATION: She doesn't back down. Not from wolves. Not from a bear.

SHOT: Title slam over black: 5 WOLVES VS 1 WOLVERINE. Cut to blue dawn light: the same snowdrift, the wolverine pushing out of the den.
NARRATION: It begins at dawn. Eight hours earlier.

## THE DEN (dawn)

SHOT: GROUND, blue dawn light: the wolverine squeezes out of a tunnel in a deep snowdrift and shakes the snow from her fur.
NARRATION: This is a wolverine. The largest weasel on land, and one of the toughest animals in the north.

SHOT: MACRO, inside the den: two small pale grey kits curled together in a hollow of snow.
NARRATION: Under the snow, she has dug a den. Her two kits are six weeks old.

SHOT: TELE: she turns back once at the entrance, then trots away across the snow.
NARRATION: They can't hunt. They can't even leave the den. Everything they eat, she has to bring back.

SHOT: MACRO: her wide furry paws pressing into the snow, barely sinking.
NARRATION: Her wide feet work like snowshoes. Where a wolf sinks, she runs.

SHOT: DRONE, sunrise: she crosses a frozen valley as the sun rises over the mountains.
NARRATION: The Yukon, Canada. Late March. The hungriest weeks of the year, when food is almost gone.

SHOT: GROUND: she stops at the edge of a spruce forest, nose up, sniffing.
NARRATION: Then she catches a smell. Fresh blood, somewhere in the trees.

## FIGHT 1 — THE LYNX (morning)

SHOT: TELE, through spruce trunks: a Canada lynx crouched over a freshly killed snowshoe hare.
NARRATION: A lynx has just killed a snowshoe hare. It's the same size as she is.

SHOT: GROUND: the wolverine walks straight toward it through the trees, head low.
NARRATION: She doesn't hide. She doesn't wait. She walks straight in.

SHOT: TELE: the lynx's ears flatten and it hisses, one paw on the hare.
NARRATION: The lynx flattens its ears and hisses. This is its kill.

SHOT: SLOW: the wolverine charges; the lynx rears up and slashes at her face with both front paws.
NARRATION: She charges. The lynx rears up and slashes at her face.

SHOT: SLOW, GROUND: they collide and roll through deep snow, a blur of grey and dark fur.
NARRATION: They crash together and roll through the snow, biting and clawing.

SHOT: MACRO: the lynx's claws raking her shoulder, sliding through thick dark fur.
NARRATION: The lynx's claws rake her shoulder. Her thick fur takes most of it.

SHOT: SLOW: she breaks free, grabs the lynx by a back leg and drags it.
NARRATION: She grabs its back leg. The lynx yowls and twists around.

SHOT: TELE: the lynx breaks away, bounds to a spruce and climbs it in two leaps.
NARRATION: It tears free and leaps up a spruce tree.

SHOT: SLOW: the wolverine climbs straight up the trunk after it, claws gripping the bark.
NARRATION: She goes up after it. Wolverines climb too.

SHOT: TELE, low angle: the lynx jumps from a high branch into the snow and bounds away into the forest.
NARRATION: The lynx jumps from the branch and runs. It wants no more of her.

SHOT: GROUND: she climbs down, picks up the hare in her jaws and trots out of the forest.
NARRATION: The first meal of the day is hers. Now she has to get it home.

## FIGHT 2 — THE GOLDEN EAGLE (midday)

SHOT: DRONE, bright midday: a tiny dark shape crossing a wide open snowfield, carrying the hare.
NARRATION: To reach the den, she has to cross open snow. There's nowhere to hide.

SHOT: TELE, against a blue sky: a golden eagle circling high above.
NARRATION: High above, a golden eagle has seen her.

SHOT: SLOW, tracking: the eagle folds its wings and dives, talons out.
NARRATION: It folds its wings and dives.

SHOT: SLOW, GROUND: the talons slam into her back; she rolls under it, snow exploding.
NARRATION: Its talons hit her back. She rolls and snaps at its legs.

SHOT: TELE: the eagle lifts off; she stands over the hare, snarling up at the sky.
NARRATION: The eagle lifts off. She stands over the hare and snarls at the sky.

SHOT: SLOW: the eagle dives again from behind, wings beating around her; she bites at a wing and feathers fly.
NARRATION: It comes again from behind, wings beating around her head. She bites off a mouthful of feathers.

SHOT: SLOW, low angle: a third dive; the eagle rips the hare from her jaws and climbs away with it.
NARRATION: On the third dive, it tears the hare out of her jaws.

SHOT: TELE: she chases the eagle's shadow across the snow, then stops, chest heaving.
NARRATION: She chases it. But she can't chase an eagle into the sky.

SHOT: GROUND: she stands alone in the middle of the snowfield with nothing in her mouth.
NARRATION: A whole morning of fighting, and she has nothing to take home.

## THE CARCASS (afternoon)

SHOT: TELE: far off over the trees, ravens circling and calling.
NARRATION: Then she hears ravens.

SHOT: TELE: she watches them, then sets off toward them at a steady run.
NARRATION: Ravens gather over meat. Hunters all across the north follow them to it.

SHOT: DRONE: she runs along a frozen creek, the ravens circling ahead.
NARRATION: She runs for six kilometres without stopping.

SHOT: TELE: in a clearing, the carcass of a bull moose lying in trampled snow, ravens hopping on it, wolf tracks everywhere.
NARRATION: A moose. Killed by wolves last night. And the wolves are gone.

SHOT: GROUND: she climbs onto the carcass and tears at a back leg. MACRO: her jaws crunching through frozen meat and bone.
NARRATION: She eats fast. Her jaws can crush meat frozen as hard as wood.

SHOT: GROUND: she rubs and sprays musk over the carcass, the ravens flapping away.
NARRATION: Then she marks it with her musk. As far as she's concerned, it's hers now.

## FIGHT 3 — THE WOLF PACK (afternoon)

SHOT: TELE: at the treeline, one grey wolf steps out, then another, then three more.
NARRATION: But the wolves only left to sleep. And now they're back.

SHOT: TELE: the five wolves trot toward the carcass, heads low, spreading out.
NARRATION: Five wolves. The pack that killed this moose.

SHOT: GROUND: she stands on top of the carcass and doesn't move.
NARRATION: Any other animal her size would run. She stays.

SHOT: SLOW: the lead wolf rushes in; she lunges and bites its muzzle; it yelps and jumps back.
NARRATION: The lead wolf rushes in. She bites its nose, and it jumps back yelping.

SHOT: TELE: the wolves begin to circle the carcass slowly, one moving in behind her.
NARRATION: The wolves start to circle. They're trying to get behind her.

SHOT: SLOW: a wolf darts in from behind and bites at her tail; she whips around and snaps.
NARRATION: One darts in from behind. She spins and snaps, and it backs off.

SHOT: SLOW: as she turns, another wolf grabs the moose and pulls; she leaps at its head.
NARRATION: Another grabs the moose and pulls. She jumps straight at its face.

SHOT: MACRO: snarling face to face, the wolverine's teeth inches from a wolf's eye.
NARRATION: One wolf could kill her. But none of them wants to be the one she bites.

SHOT: Stillness. TELE: all five wolves stop around the carcass, panting; she stands panting on top.
NARRATION: For a moment, everyone stops. Their breath steams in the cold air.

SHOT: SLOW: two wolves attack from both sides at once and knock her off the carcass into the snow.
NARRATION: Then two come at once, from both sides. She's knocked into the snow.

SHOT: SLOW, GROUND: a wolf grabs her by the scruff and lifts her off the ground; she twists and bites its leg.
NARRATION: A wolf grabs her by the neck and lifts her. She twists, and bites deep into its leg.

SHOT: TELE: the wolf drops her with a yelp; she scrambles straight back onto the carcass.
NARRATION: It drops her. She climbs straight back onto the moose.

SHOT: SLOW: three wolves lunge together; she rips a strip of meat free, leaps off and races for a spruce.
NARRATION: Now three come together. This time she grabs a mouthful of meat and runs.

SHOT: SLOW: she scrambles up the trunk just ahead of snapping jaws.
NARRATION: Jaws snap shut just below her feet. She's up the tree.

SHOT: TELE: the five wolves circle the base of the tree; she lies along a branch above them, eating.
NARRATION: The wolves circle below. Above them, she eats.

SHOT: TELE, low sun turning the snow gold: the wolves lie down around the tree and the carcass.
NARRATION: Wolves can wait for hours. And they won't leave their kill.

## FIGHT 4 — THE GRIZZLY (sunset)

SHOT: TELE, golden sunset: all five wolves suddenly stand, fur bristling, staring at the treeline.
NARRATION: At sunset, the wolves stand up, all at once. Their fur rises.

SHOT: TELE: out of the spruce trees, a huge grizzly bear walks slowly toward the carcass, thin from winter.
NARRATION: A grizzly. Just woken from five months asleep, and starving.

SHOT: SLOW: the bear charges; the wolves scatter in every direction.
NARRATION: It charges. All five wolves scatter.

SHOT: GROUND: the bear stands over the moose, tearing at it, breath steaming.
NARRATION: The moose belongs to the bear now. It weighs twenty times more than she does.

SHOT: TELE: the wolverine climbs slowly down from the tree, eyes on the bear.
NARRATION: And she climbs down from the tree.

SHOT: GROUND: she walks straight up to the carcass, snarling, and grabs the back leg she was tearing at earlier.
NARRATION: She walks right up to it, and grabs the leg she started on this afternoon.

SHOT: SLOW: the bear swings its head and roars in her face; she snarls back without moving.
NARRATION: The bear roars in her face. She roars back.

SHOT: SLOW: the bear swats her with one huge paw and she flies into the snow, rolling.
NARRATION: One swipe of its paw throws her into the snow.

SHOT: SLOW, GROUND: she gets up and runs straight back in, biting the bear's front paw.
NARRATION: She gets up. She goes straight back in, and bites its paw.

SHOT: TELE: the bear rears onto its hind legs, towering over her; she stands her ground, teeth bared.
NARRATION: The bear rears up on its back legs. She doesn't move.

SHOT: SLOW: the bear drops down onto her; she darts aside and its paws slam into the snow where she stood.
NARRATION: It crashes down. She's already gone.

SHOT: SLOW: as the bear turns, she rips the leg free from the carcass at the joint.
NARRATION: While it turns, she tears the leg free at the joint.

SHOT: TELE: she drags the leg, almost her own size, into the darkening trees while the bear stares after her.
NARRATION: A leg almost as big as she is. And she drags it into the dark.

## ENDING (night)

SHOT: DRONE, LOW-LIGHT: green northern lights over the valley; a tiny dark shape dragging something across the snow.
NARRATION: She drags it for hours, for kilometres. Under the northern lights.

SHOT: GROUND, LOW-LIGHT: she reaches the snowdrift and pulls the leg into the den tunnel.
NARRATION: Near midnight, she reaches the snowdrift.

SHOT: MACRO, inside the den: the two kits crawl over the meat and start to eat; she curls up around them.
NARRATION: Her kits are there. Both of them. And tonight, they eat.

SHOT: MACRO: her face, scratched, frost on her fur, her eyes slowly closing.
NARRATION: A lynx. An eagle. Five wolves. A grizzly. She never backed down from any of them.

SHOT: TELE, LOW-LIGHT, under the aurora: in the valley below, a line of huge bear tracks heading toward the snowdrift.
NARRATION: But down in the valley, the bear has found her trail.

SHOT: Black. One final hit.
NARRATION: If she earned your respect, hit subscribe. There are more battles like this coming.
