# HARPY EAGLES NEVER MISS — Visual Analysis (State 7)

One day in the Amazon: a mother spider monkey with her baby on her back is hunted through the treetops by a family of three harpy eagles, from the canopy to a river jump to a thicket of spiny palms. It should look like real wildlife footage from start to finish: telephoto from a hidden position, a camera on the branches at monkey height, macro, slow motion and aerial shots, all in natural daylight.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** photorealistic wildlife documentary footage, as if filmed by a professional natural-history crew, 16:9 widescreen. Never CGI, cartoon or fantasy.
- **Lighting (every beat):** natural daylight only. True-to-life colours, real shadows, realistic exposure. No colour grade, no golden or orange tint, no rim light, no light beams, no mist glow, no studio lighting. The time of day changes only as real daylight does.
- **Palette:**
  - the deep greens of the rainforest canopy, grey and brown tree trunks, a pale blue sky with white clouds above the treetops,
  - a wide muddy-brown river,
  - the spider monkey's reddish-brown to black fur and pale face skin, the baby's darker fluffy fur,
  - the harpy eagles' slate-grey head and crest, black chest band, white belly, black-and-grey barred wings and legs, and huge yellow feet,
  - a natural, neutral grade.
- **Light by section (all natural):**
  - **Cold open:** clear natural midday light under a blue sky (matches the thumbnail), with flashes of the other scenes in their own light.
  - **Left behind:** natural morning daylight in the canopy, soft shadows.
  - **The treetop:** bright natural late-morning light above the canopy, open blue sky.
  - **The vines:** natural dappled light inside the forest, green shade with patches of sun.
  - **The gap:** natural afternoon daylight over the river, a pale sky with scattered clouds.
  - **The palms:** natural late-afternoon light, deeper shade inside the thicket.
  - **Together:** soft natural early-evening light, before sunset.
- **Camera key** (as in the script):
  - **TELE:** a 400–800mm long lens from a hidden position, shallow focus, leaves blurred in the foreground.
  - **GROUND:** a camera fixed on a branch at monkey height, with leaves and bark in the foreground and animals passing close to the lens.
  - **MACRO:** faces, eyes, hands, tails, talons, the baby.
  - **SLOW:** 120 fps for every leap, strike, fall, swing and snap.
  - **DRONE:** wide shots of the canopy and the river only.
- **Composition:**
  - **The chase:** wide, side-on shots with the mother above or ahead of the eagle and a clear gap of sky or leaves between them.
  - **The baby:** always visible on her back, or, in the river scene, hanging by its tail.
  - **The eagles:** coming from above and behind, wings wide.
- **Realism:**
  - natural film grain and imperfect focus in action shots,
  - leaves, bark, vines and branches moving with real physics,
  - eagle feathers ruffling in flight, the monkeys' long limbs and tails moving naturally.
- **No gore:** the talons never connect with a monkey. Every strike misses and hits air, bark, leaves, vines or palm spines. No blood, no injuries.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **The mother:** an adult black-headed spider monkey with long, thin arms and legs, reddish-brown to black fur, a small round head with a pale bare face, and a very long prehensile tail with a bare gripping pad at its tip.
- **The baby:** a four-month-old baby spider monkey with dark fluffy fur, a tiny pale face and big dark eyes, riding on its mother's back with its tail wrapped around the base of hers.
- **The female harpy eagle:** a huge female harpy eagle with a slate-grey head and a split crest of long grey feathers, a black band across her chest, a white belly, black-and-grey barred wings, thick barred legs and enormous yellow feet with long black talons.
- **The male harpy eagle:** a smaller male harpy eagle with the same slate-grey head and crest, black chest band, white belly, barred wings and yellow feet with black talons.
- **The young eagle:** a juvenile harpy eagle with a pale whitish-grey head and crest, a pale body, light grey wings and yellow feet, clumsy in flight.
- **The troop:** about twenty spider monkeys with long limbs, dark fur and long tails.

## Locations
1. **The canopy:** the upper branches of the Amazon rainforest, with a fig tree full of fruit.
2. **The giant tree:** a tall emergent tree rising above the rest of the canopy, with an open crown and sky all around.
3. **The vines:** a dense curtain of hanging vines and lianas between the trees.
4. **The river:** a wide, muddy-brown river with tall trees on both banks and one dead tree standing in the middle.
5. **The palms:** a dense thicket of palms with spiny trunks and thick fronds on the far riverbank.

## Rules
- Every prompt stands alone, with the full character description, location and lighting.
- No text, logos, people, boats, buildings, roads or gore.
- Correct anatomy: spider monkeys with two long arms, two long legs and one long tail; eagles with two wings and two legs; an exact number of animals per shot.
- Scale: the female harpy is clearly bigger than the male; a monkey is about the size of the female eagle's body; the baby is tiny on its mother's back.
- Image and video prompts use the Wild Ruaha style: script text, a full prompt, then camera, lighting, mood and action lines (video: camera movement, subject motion, environment motion, duration).
