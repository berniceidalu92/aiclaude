# WILD HIMALAYA — Visual Analysis (State 7)

The same locked Nature's Coliseum look, adapted to a frozen high-altitude cliff world. It is cold and blue-white, very different from the golden Ruaha video.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** Photorealistic cinematic wildlife documentary with a premium nature-film look, 16:9 widescreen, AI-rendered to feel like high-end telephoto footage.
- **Palette:** Cool blue-white snow and pale grey-brown rock, with dark slate cliff faces and steel-blue shadows. The only warm colors are a low pale-gold winter sun and the warm tones of the animals: the ibex's sandy-brown coat, the snow leopard's smoky grey with black rosettes, and the wolves' tawny grey. The grade is cool and crisp, with clean highlights, deep blue shadows and moderate saturation.
- **Lighting:** Low winter sun with hard golden rim light on fur and horns. Spindrift (blowing snow) and breath vapor catch the light, and the high-altitude air is very clear.
- **Camera:**
  - Long telephoto lens (300–800mm feel), very shallow depth of field, compressed perspective that stacks cliffs and peaks.
  - Shots across a valley at cliff level, and shots looking straight down a cliff face to show the drop.
  - Fast shutter freezing leaps, falls, horn clashes, flying snow and rock.
  - Extreme close-ups for eyes, hooves and horns.
- **Composition:**
  - Animals small against huge vertical rock in the wide shots, to show the scale and the drop.
  - In ledge fights, always frame the drop beside the animal, so viewers feel that one slip kills.
  - In the snow fights, frame low across the snow surface to show the ibex sinking while the wolves stay on top.
- **Detail:** Ultra-fine detail in focus (fur, frost on whiskers and beards, horn ridges, hoof edges, snow crystals, rock grain) and creamy bokeh everywhere else.
- **Mood:** Tense, vertiginous, cold, majestic. Fights are visceral without gore.

## Real-documentary feel (every beat)
- Every shot should look like footage filmed by a natural-history camera crew from a hidden position at long range, not like CGI or a staged render.
- The animals are wild, unposed and unaware of the camera. Every pose, gait and expression is one the real animal makes.
- True-to-life anatomy, proportions, fur and horn texture, and natural colors without oversaturation.
- The small imperfections of real telephoto footage: atmospheric haze over distance, fine film grain, gentle lens breathing and focus pulls.
- Video: a steady tripod or gimbal-mounted long lens. Action plays in true high-frame-rate slow motion with real weight and physics for snow, rock and wind.

## Continuity between beats
- Each scene has one fixed time of day and lighting from its first beat to its last. Beats never switch lighting mid-scene.
- Generate the first beat of each scene first, then use the previous beat's image as the reference for the next one, so the animals, location and light carry straight on.
- Injuries carry forward once they happen: the young male's leg gash (from the rut), Nima's shoulder bite (from the wolves) and Kala's leg bites (from the ledge).
- Each teaser shot matches the story beat it previews, with the same animals, lighting and setting.
- The image and video prompt files give each beat a "Continuity" line with its scene, its shot number and the beat to use as the reference.

## Lighting variations
- **Dawn:** pink-gold alpenglow on the peaks, the valley still in blue shadow.
- **Day (eagle scenes):** bright high-altitude sun and a deep blue sky. The eagle dives out of the sun with lens flare.
- **Dusk (final hunt, climax opener):** the last orange light on the ridgelines, blue twilight on the snow, breath steaming.
- **Night (wolves vs snow leopards):** soft silver moonlight on snow and the frozen river, glowing eyes and a faint cold rim light on fur.
- **Storm:** heavy falling snow, flat grey light, low visibility.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **Kala:** a huge old male Himalayan ibex with a thick sandy-brown winter coat, a dark beard, a scarred face and massive curved horns over a meter long, ridged like a saw blade and swept back over his shoulders. After the final hunt, he has wolf bite marks on his back legs.
- **Nima:** a female Himalayan ibex with a lighter sandy-grey coat, short curved horns and a slim, agile build.
- **Tashi:** a small young Himalayan ibex kid with a soft pale-grey coat and tiny horn buds.
- **The young male:** a powerful young male Himalayan ibex with a sandy-brown coat and long ridged horns, slightly shorter than Kala's. After the rut, he has a bleeding gash on his leg.
- **Dolma:** an adult female snow leopard with a thick smoky-grey coat covered in black rosettes, pale green-grey eyes, and a long thick tail almost as long as her body.
- **The cubs:** two grown snow leopard cubs, almost adult size, with the same smoky-grey coats, slightly lankier and more clumsy.
- **The wolves:** a pack of lean Himalayan wolves with thick tawny-grey winter coats, pale chests and yellow eyes.
- **The grey female:** the lead wolf, a lean she-wolf with a pale grey coat and a dark streak down her back.
- **The golden eagles:** large golden eagles with dark brown feathers, golden napes and huge yellow talons.

## Locations
1. **The cliff face:** sheer slate-grey rock walls with ledges no wider than a hand, hundreds of meters above the valley.
2. **The scree slopes:** steep slopes of loose grey rock and patches of snow below the cliffs, where the rut fight happens.
3. **The gully:** a narrow rocky gully below a wall of rock, where Dolma ambushes the young male.
4. **The ice slope:** a steep slope of frozen, glassy snow with a long drop to the valley floor.
5. **The valley slopes:** wide open slopes of deep fresh snow with dry grass poking through, below the cliffs.
6. **The frozen river:** a wide river covered in ice and snow at the valley floor, with boulders along its banks.
7. **The ledge:** a ledge no wider than a doorway at the end of a ridge, above a 200-meter drop, with a thin crack in the cliff face five meters below it.
8. **Background:** the Spiti Valley in the Indian Himalaya, with huge snow peaks, a cold desert of bare brown-grey mountains and a deep blue sky.

## Rules
- Every prompt is standalone: full character description, location and style every time.
- No text, no logos, no music, no human presence, villages or vehicles, and no gore.
- Fights are frozen at the moment of contact (horn clashes, leaps, talon strikes, falls, snow and rock flying), with no focus on blood. Wounds are only suggested (a gash, a limp, bite marks).
- Always show the drop in cliff and ledge shots, so viewers feel the height.
- In wolf scenes, show the ibex sinking in the snow while the wolves run on top of it.
