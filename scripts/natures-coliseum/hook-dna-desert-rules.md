## Rules to copy
1. Open on a secret rule plus a paradox, not on scenery.
2. Plant a third, hidden threat in the first 40 seconds and pay it off 2–3 minutes later.
3. Every 10–20 seconds, one short line (4–12 words) that is a fact, a flip, a rule or a cliffhanger.
4. Turn every victory into new danger ("winning is an announcement").
5. End every section on a one-line cliffhanger.
6. Use contrast pairs and stat-then-subversion lines.
7. Let the footage carry the action; narration only names what it means.
8. Add an out-of-nowhere twist late (the barbed wire), then a loyalty or rescue payoff.
9. Do NOT copy the second half's encyclopedia format. That is where hooks drop off.
