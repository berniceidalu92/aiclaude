One bite. Then he waits.

On a remote island in Indonesia, a Komodo dragon takes on a water buffalo seven times his weight, and wins with patience. Meet Naga, a giant dragon who bites once and then follows his prey for three days while his venom does the work.

But the scent of blood draws an even bigger rival out of the hills. And high in the trees, a young dragon must survive a world where the greatest danger is her own kind.

So how does a hunter that cannot overpower its prey bring down an animal seven times its size?

🦎 In this documentary:
• How Komodo dragon venom really works (the bacteria story is a myth)
• A three-day hunt through the hills, waterholes and mangroves of Rinca
• Why baby Komodo dragons live in the trees
• Two giant dragons rise up on their hind legs to fight for a kill
• Why only a few thousand Komodo dragons remain on Earth
