# 5 WOLVES CORNER A WOLVERINE — Visual Analysis (State 7)

One day in the Yukon, late March: a mother wolverine leaves her two kits in a snow den and fights four hunters in a row, from a lynx to a grizzly, to bring food home. It should look like real wildlife footage from start to finish: telephoto from a hidden position, low remote cameras in the snow, macro, slow motion, and low-light footage under the northern lights.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** photorealistic wildlife documentary footage, as if filmed by a professional natural-history crew, 16:9 widescreen. Never CGI, cartoon or fantasy.
- **Palette:**
  - bright white snow, dark green spruce forest, grey rock and pale blue ice,
  - a clear deep-blue sky by day, gold at sunset, green aurora at night,
  - the wolverine's dark brown fur with its pale golden-tan side stripes, the lynx's silver-grey, the wolves' grey and cream, the grizzly's brown with silver-tipped hump,
  - a natural, bright, clean grade.
- **Lighting by section, following the real time of day:**
  - **Cold open:** bright afternoon snow under a clear blue sky (matches the thumbnail), with flashes of the other fights in their own light.
  - **The den:** blue dawn light, then the sun rising over the mountains.
  - **Fight 1, the lynx:** soft morning sun through spruce trunks, shafts of light, snow glittering.
  - **Fight 2, the eagle:** hard bright midday sun on an open snowfield, a deep blue sky, crisp shadows.
  - **The carcass and Fight 3, the wolves:** bright afternoon sun, sliding into low golden light near the end.
  - **Fight 4, the grizzly:** golden sunset, long blue shadows, breath and snow glowing in the backlight.
  - **The ending:** night under green northern lights, starlight on the snow, slight grain. Keep it colourful, never black-and-white.
- **Camera key** (as in the script):
  - **TELE:** a 400–800mm long lens from a hidden position, shallow focus, snow crystals drifting in front.
  - **GROUND:** a low remote camera at wolverine height, with snow in the foreground and animals passing close to the lens.
  - **MACRO:** teeth, paws, claws, eyes, frost on fur, the kits' faces.
  - **SLOW:** 120 fps for every lunge, bite, dive, swipe and fall.
  - **DRONE:** wide shots of valleys, snowfields, the creek and the aurora only.
  - **LOW-LIGHT:** night scenes under the aurora.
- **Composition:**
  - **The wolverine:** small, dark and sharp at the centre, the hunters crowding the frame around her.
  - **Size:** always show the gap. Wolves and the bear tower over her.
  - **The kits:** shown only at the den, to keep the stakes clear.
- **Realism:**
  - natural film grain and imperfect focus in action shots,
  - snow spraying and breath steaming with real physics,
  - fur that gets wet, clumped and frosted as the day goes on.
- **No gore:** bites are shown at the moment of contact, with no blood in focus. The moose carcass is shown as a dark, frozen shape in trampled snow, never opened or bloody. The wolverine ends scratched and frosted, never wounded.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **The wolverine:** a female wolverine with thick, shaggy dark brown fur, a pale golden-tan stripe running along each side from shoulder to tail, a pale band across her forehead, a broad head with small rounded ears and small dark eyes, short powerful legs, wide furry paws with long pale claws, and a bushy tail.
- **The kits:** two six-week-old wolverine kits with fluffy pale grey-cream fur, dark faces and tiny dark eyes.
- **The lynx:** a Canada lynx with thick silver-grey fur, long black ear tufts, a ruff of fur around its face, long legs, huge furry paws and a short black-tipped tail.
- **The golden eagle:** a golden eagle with dark brown plumage, golden feathers on the back of its head, a hooked yellow-based beak, yellow feet and huge black talons.
- **The wolves:** five grey wolves with thick grey and cream fur, darker backs, pale faces and yellow eyes. One large dark-grey male leads the pack.
- **The grizzly:** a huge brown grizzly bear, thin after winter, with a big shoulder hump, silver-tipped fur on its back and long pale claws.
- **The ravens:** common ravens, large and glossy black.
- **The snowshoe hare:** a white snowshoe hare (shown only as the kill in the lynx's paws or the wolverine's jaws, never torn).

## Locations
1. **The den:** a deep snowdrift on a slope above the valley, with a small dark tunnel entrance.
2. **The frozen valley:** a wide white valley between snow-covered mountains.
3. **The spruce forest:** tall, dark spruce trees with deep snow between the trunks.
4. **The open snowfield:** a wide, flat, empty sheet of snow with nowhere to hide.
5. **The frozen creek:** a winding creek covered in ice and snow through the trees.
6. **The clearing:** an open patch of trampled snow at the forest edge where the moose carcass lies, with one tall spruce at its edge.

## Rules
- Every prompt stands alone, with the full character description, location and lighting.
- No text, logos, people, vehicles, buildings, roads, fences or gore.
- Correct anatomy: four legs per mammal, one tail, an exact number of animals per shot (always exactly five wolves when the pack is shown).
- Scale: the wolverine and the lynx are about the same body size (the lynx taller on its long legs); every wolf is far bigger, and the grizzly dwarfs them all.
- Image and video prompts use the Wild Ruaha style: script text, a full prompt, then camera, lighting, mood and action lines (video: camera movement, subject motion, environment motion, duration).
