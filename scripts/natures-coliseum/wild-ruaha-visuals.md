# WILD RUAHA — Visual Analysis (State 7)

Same locked Nature's Coliseum look, adapted to a drought-season African riverbed.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** Photorealistic cinematic wildlife documentary, premium nature-film look, 16:9 widescreen, AI-rendered to feel like high-end telephoto footage.
- **Palette:** Warm honey-gold and amber light over pale golden sand, dusty ochre earth, dry straw-colored grass, grey-brown baobab trunks and a few last pools of green-brown water. Tawny lion fur and the giraffe's chestnut-and-cream patchwork are the brightest colors in frame. A soft warm golden grade with gently lifted blacks and moderate saturation.
- **Lighting:** Low golden-hour sun, backlit rim light glowing on fur and manes, dust hanging in the air and catching the light, heat haze over the sand.
- **Camera:** Long telephoto lens (200–600mm feel), very shallow depth of field, compressed perspective. Camera low at lion eye level, looking up at the giraffe to exaggerate its height. Fast shutter freezing kicks, leaps, sand and dust. Extreme close-ups for emotional beats.
- **Composition:** Subject in a narrow sharp focal plane, framed by blurred foreground grass. In giraffe fights, frame tall enough to show the whole giraffe with lions on it. Wide shots place animals low against the sand river and baobabs.
- **Detail:** Ultra-fine micro-detail in focus (fur, whiskers, claws, hooves, sand grains, dust) and creamy bokeh everywhere else.
- **Mood:** Intense, powerful, majestic. Visceral in fights, without gore.

## Lighting exceptions
- **Night (hyena scenes):** soft silver moonlight with a faint warm rim on fur, glowing hyena eyes, dust in the moonlight.
- **Dawn:** pale gold light with low mist over the riverbed.
- **Midday:** harsh bright sun with heavy heat haze (the young male's solo attack).

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **Zuri:** an older, heavily built African lioness with a tawny coat, a scarred face, a torn lower lip and amber eyes.
- **Kesi:** a young, lean two-year-old African lioness with a light golden coat, a slim build and bright amber eyes. After the first kick, she moves stiffly with a bruise on her ribs.
- **Jabari:** a huge male African lion with a thick black-tipped dark mane, a heavy muscular build and amber eyes. After the kick to the head, he has a swollen jaw.
- **The young male:** a three-year-old male lion with a short, scruffy growing mane and a lean body.
- **The Tower:** a giant old bull giraffe nearly six meters tall, with dark chestnut patches on a cream background, a thick heavily scarred neck and large knobbly ossicones.
- **The mother giraffe and calf:** a lighter-patched female giraffe with a small young calf with soft pale patches.
- **The hyenas:** a large clan of spotted hyenas with sloping backs, rounded ears and glowing eyes.
- **The crocodile:** a four-meter Nile crocodile with dark olive scales and pale yellow eyes.
- **The cubs:** three small lion cubs with faint spots on their fur.

## Locations
1. **The dry riverbed:** a wide channel of pale soft sand where the Great Ruaha River used to flow, cracked mud at its edges.
2. **The last pools:** a few green-brown pools of water in the sand, crocodiles lying in them.
3. **The riverbank:** dry golden grass, thorn bushes and giant baobab trees.
4. **Open hard ground:** flat, firm, dusty earth (where the giraffe escapes).
5. **The rocky den:** a pile of boulders above the river with a small cave for the cubs.

## Rules
- Every prompt is standalone: full character description, location and style every time.
- No text, no logos, no music, no human presence or vehicles, no gore.
- Fights are frozen at the moment of contact (kicks, leaps, claws, sand flying), with no blood focus.
- Always show the giraffe's full height when lions are on it, so the size difference is clear.
