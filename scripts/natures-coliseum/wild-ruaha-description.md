## Description (paste into YouTube)

Eight lions. One giant giraffe. Only one side walks away.

In the dry season, the Great Ruaha River in Tanzania turns to sand, and a starving pride takes on the most dangerous prey a lion can hunt: a six-meter bull giraffe whose single kick can kill a lion outright. One lion fails. Then three. Then four, five and seven. Hyenas, a crocodile and a giant they call the Tower stand between Zuri's pride and survival, until she finds the one place and the one moment where a giraffe cannot fight back.

Ruaha National Park is one of the last great strongholds for lions in Africa. But the Great Ruaha River now runs dry every year, and when the river dies, everything that depends on it is pushed to the edge. Even lions.
