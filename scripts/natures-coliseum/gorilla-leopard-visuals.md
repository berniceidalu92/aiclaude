# A LEOPARD HUNTS A GORILLA FAMILY — Visual Analysis (State 7)

One day and one night in the Congo rainforest: a silverback gorilla protects his family and a two-week-old baby from four hunters in a row, from a forest elephant to a leopard. It should look like real wildlife footage from start to finish: telephoto from a hidden position, low remote cameras on the forest floor, macro, slow motion, and low-light footage at night.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** photorealistic wildlife documentary footage, as if filmed by a professional natural-history crew, 16:9 widescreen. Never CGI, cartoon or fantasy.
- **Palette:**
  - deep green rainforest, golden shafts of light, morning mist, red-brown mud and shallow silver pools in the clearing,
  - the silverback's black fur with its silver-grey saddle, the leopard's golden rosetted coat, the chimpanzees' black fur and pale faces, the elephant's grey mud-caked skin,
  - a natural, bright, warm grade.
- **Lighting by section, following the real time of day:**
  - **Cold open:** golden dawn light through mist in the forest (matches the thumbnail), with flashes of the other fights in their own light.
  - **The family and Fight 1, the elephant:** soft sunrise over the open clearing, mist on the water, golden light.
  - **Fight 2, the chimpanzees:** bright midday light falling in shafts through the canopy, dappled green shade.
  - **Fight 3, the rival:** warm afternoon light in the forest, dappled sun on fur and leaves.
  - **Night:** a golden sunset over the canopy, then night under a faint moon: deep blue shadows, a soft silver rim on fur, leopard eyeshine. Keep the colour, never black-and-white.
  - **Fight 4, the leopard, and the ending:** grey first light turning to golden dawn, with bright shafts of sun through the mist. Keep it bright.
- **Camera key** (as in the script):
  - **TELE:** a 400–800mm long lens from a hidden position, shallow focus, leaves blurred in the foreground.
  - **GROUND:** a low remote camera on the forest floor, with leaves and roots in the foreground and animals passing close to the lens.
  - **MACRO:** faces, eyes, hands, teeth, the baby's face and hands.
  - **SLOW:** 120 fps for every charge, chest beat, swipe, leap and fall.
  - **DRONE:** wide shots of the canopy, the clearing and the river only.
  - **LOW-LIGHT:** night scenes.
- **Composition:**
  - **The silverback:** large, dark and sharp at the centre, always between the danger and his family.
  - **The baby:** shown in close-ups on its mother's chest to keep the stakes clear.
  - **The enemies:** coming from the edges of the frame, the trees and above.
- **Realism:**
  - natural film grain and imperfect focus in action shots,
  - mud, water, leaves and branches moving with real physics,
  - fur that gets muddy and scratched as the day goes on.
- **No gore:** every fight shows contact, never wounds. No blood, no open injuries. The silverback ends up muddy and scratched. The chimpanzee grabs the mother, never the baby. Nothing is ever eaten.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **The silverback:** a huge adult male western lowland gorilla with thick black fur, a broad silver-grey saddle across his back, a tall crested head, a massive chest and shoulders, long powerful arms, dark brown eyes and long canine teeth.
- **The mother:** an adult female western lowland gorilla with black fur, smaller than the silverback, with a two-week-old baby clinging to her chest.
- **The baby:** a two-week-old baby gorilla with soft black fur, a tiny pale-pink face, big dark eyes and tiny hands gripping its mother's fur.
- **The females:** three adult female western lowland gorillas with black fur (one of them the mother).
- **The forest elephant:** a forest elephant bull with grey, mud-caked skin, small rounded ears and straight, downward-pointing tusks.
- **The chimpanzees:** wild chimpanzees with black fur, pale faces, big ears and long arms, their hair standing on end. A gang of about twenty-five.
- **The rival:** a younger adult male western lowland gorilla with black fur and a narrower silver-grey saddle, leaner than the old silverback.
- **The leopard:** an adult African leopard with a golden coat covered in black rosettes, pale yellow-green eyes and a long tail.

## Locations
1. **The clearing:** a wide open clearing in the rainforest with shallow pools, green grass and red-brown mud, surrounded by a wall of tall trees.
2. **The forest:** tall rainforest trees with buttress roots, hanging vines and a forest floor of leaves.
3. **The fig tree:** a huge fig tree heavy with ripe fruit, its branches reaching over the forest floor.
4. **The nest site:** a patch of forest floor among bushes and tree trunks, with nests of bent leafy branches.
5. **Background:** the endless green canopy of the Congo Basin with a winding river and morning mist.

## Rules
- Every prompt stands alone, with the full character description, location and lighting.
- No text, logos, people, vehicles, villages, roads or gore.
- Correct anatomy: gorillas and chimpanzees with two arms, two legs and no tail; four legs and one tail for the leopard; an exact number of animals per shot.
- Scale: the silverback is far bigger than the leopard, every chimpanzee and the rival; the elephant dwarfs them all.
- Image and video prompts use the Wild Ruaha style: script text, a full prompt, then camera, lighting, mood and action lines (video: camera movement, subject motion, environment motion, duration).
