# WILD SUNDARBANS — Visual Analysis (State 7)

Same locked Nature's Coliseum look as Wild Pantanal, adapted to a tidal mangrove forest.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** Photorealistic cinematic wildlife documentary, premium nature-film look, 16:9 widescreen, AI-rendered to feel like high-end telephoto footage.
- **Palette:** Warm honey-gold and amber light over deep mangrove greens, wet silver-grey mud, and murky brown-green tidal water. Orange-and-black tiger stripes are the brightest color in every frame. A soft warm golden grade with gently lifted blacks and moderate saturation.
- **Lighting:** Low-angle golden-hour sunlight, backlit rim light on fur and whiskers, sparkling highlights on wet mud and water, soft river mist and humid haze.
- **Camera:** Long telephoto lens (200–600mm feel), very shallow depth of field, compressed perspective. Camera low, at the animal's eye level or at the waterline (half-submerged angles in swimming scenes). Extreme close-ups for emotional beats.
- **Composition:** Subject in a narrow sharp focal plane, framed by blurred foreground roots and leaves. Wide shots place subjects low, with layered mangrove walls fading into mist above.
- **Detail:** Ultra-fine micro-detail in focus (wet fur, whiskers, droplets, mud on paws, crocodile scales, snake scales) and creamy bokeh everywhere else.
- **Mood:** Intimate, reverent, majestic. Visceral in fights, without gore.

## Lighting exceptions (scene-specific)
- **Cyclone (night):** dark blue-grey storm light, lightning flashes, driving sideways rain, churning black water. The only cool-toned section.
- **Night alone / moonlight:** silver moonlight with a faint warm rim on fur.
- **Morning after the storm:** pale gold light through mist over broken trees.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **Rani:** an adult Bengal tigress, lean and muscular, with a deep orange coat, dense dark black stripes, a white muzzle and chest, amber eyes, legs stained grey with mud. Later scenes add a fresh wound on her left shoulder.
- **Ravi:** a ten-month-old male Bengal tiger cub, stocky and bold, with fluffy orange fur, bold black stripes, oversized paws and bright curious amber eyes.
- **Nila:** a ten-month-old female Bengal tiger cub, smaller and slimmer than her brother, with soft orange fur, fine black stripes, and watchful pale amber eyes.
- **Torn Ear:** a large old Bengal tigress, heavier than Rani, with a faded orange coat, thick black stripes, a scarred face and her left ear torn half away.
- **The crocodile:** a huge saltwater crocodile, over five meters long, with dark grey-olive armoured scales, a broad scarred snout and pale yellow eyes.
- **The king cobra:** a long olive-brown king cobra with faint pale bands, its hood spread wide, raised a third of its body off the ground.
- **The bull shark:** a stocky grey bull shark with a blunt snout, its dorsal fin cutting murky brown water.
- **Others:** spotted deer (chital) with white-spotted chestnut coats, rhesus macaques, fiddler crabs with one giant claw, mudskippers, a two-meter water monitor.

## Locations
1. **Tidal channels:** wide brown-green water lined with dense mangrove walls, mist above.
2. **Low-tide mudflats:** shining grey mud full of crabs and mudskippers.
3. **Mangrove roots:** spiky breathing roots (pneumatophores) poking up out of the mud like stakes.
4. **Forest interior at high tide:** water flooding between the trees, with the canopy above.
5. **Storm-wrecked forest:** snapped trees, debris rafts, new channels cut through the land.

## Rules
- Every prompt is standalone: full character description, location and style every time.
- No text, no logos, no music, no human presence, no gore.
- Fights are frozen at the moment of contact (spray, mud, claws), with no blood focus.
