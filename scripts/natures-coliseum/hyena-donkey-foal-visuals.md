# STRIPED HYENA vs DONKEY FOAL — Visual Analysis (State 7)

One night in the dry thornbush of northern Kenya: a mother donkey and her two-week-old foal against a striped hyena, black-backed jackals and two spotted hyenas. The style is cinematic and emotional, with the footage carrying the action between short narration lines.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** photorealistic cinematic wildlife documentary, premium nature-film look, 16:9 widescreen. Real footage, never CGI.
- **Palette:**
  - dry golden-brown grass, grey thornbush, red-brown dust, pale acacias,
  - a burning orange and violet dusk sky, turning to deep blue night with a big moon,
  - the mother donkey's soft grey coat and the foal's fluffy pale grey-brown coat,
  - the striped hyena's pale coat with black stripes and a dark bristling mane,
  - natural, warm-to-cool grade as the night falls.
- **Lighting by section:**
  - **Opening and first chase:** low dusk sun, long shadows and golden rim light, dust glowing in the light.
  - **Jackals and spotted hyenas:** last light of dusk into blue hour, silhouettes against the orange horizon.
  - **The tall grass and the fury fight:** moonlight with a soft silver rim on fur, glowing eyes, dust in the moonbeams.
  - **Reunion and ending:** moonlight and the first hint of dawn on the horizon.
- **Camera:**
  - long telephoto (300–600mm) for stalks and watching,
  - low ground-level shots in the grass for the hunter's point of view,
  - close tracking for chases, kicks and the fight,
  - true slow motion for kicks, leaps and dust explosions,
  - close-ups of eyes, ears, hooves and the mane.
- **Composition:**
  - **The gap:** frame mother and foal with the visible gap between them growing in each shot.
  - **The hunter:** keep it in the foreground grass, out of focus, with the foal sharp beyond it.
  - **Scale:** show the mother big and the foal small.
- **Detail:** fur, bristling mane hairs, hoof dust, grass seed heads, sweat and breath in the cold night air.
- **Mood:**
  - playful and innocent at the start,
  - then tense, frantic and terrifying,
  - then silent and heartbreaking,
  - then furious,
  - then tender and quiet at the end.
- **No gore:** bites and kicks are shown at the moment of contact, with no blood in focus. The foal's injury is only scrapes and a limp.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **The mother (the mountain):** a large adult donkey with a soft grey coat, a pale muzzle and belly, a dark stripe along her back and across her shoulders, long upright ears with dark tips, and a short upright mane.
- **The foal (the ember):** a two-week-old donkey foal with a fluffy pale grey-brown coat, oversized long ears, big dark eyes and long wobbly legs.
- **The striped hyena (the ghost):** a striped hyena with a pale grey-buff coat, black vertical stripes on its body and legs, a long dark mane along its back that bristles up when it attacks, pointed ears and dark eyes.
- **The jackals (the whispers):** black-backed jackals with ginger-red legs and flanks, a saddle of silver-flecked black fur across the back, big pointed ears and bushy black-tipped tails.
- **The spotted hyenas (the iron jaws):** two large spotted hyenas with sandy coats covered in dark spots, heavy shoulders, sloping backs, rounded ears and massive jaws.
- **The vultures (the undertakers):** white-backed vultures with broad wings and bald, wrinkled heads.

## Locations
1. **The thornbush edge:** dry golden grass meeting grey acacia thornbush, with scattered flat-topped acacias.
2. **The open plain:** flat, dusty open ground with sparse grass, where the chases happen.
3. **The tall grass (the maze):** a dense stand of tall dry grass higher than the mother's back, with a trampled hollow inside.
4. **The hyena's cave:** a rocky outcrop with a dark cave mouth, at dusk.
5. **Background:** the dry thornbush of northern Kenya, with distant rocky hills, a huge open sky and a big moon.

## Rules
- Every prompt stands alone, with the full character description, location and lighting.
- No text, logos, people, villages, roads, vehicles or gore.
- Correct anatomy: four legs per animal, one tail, no extra animals beyond those written in the prompt.
- Teaser shots (beats 1–7) use the same look as the story beats they preview.
- Image and video prompts use the Wild Ruaha style: script text, a full prompt, then camera, lighting, mood and action lines (video: camera movement, subject motion, environment motion, duration).
