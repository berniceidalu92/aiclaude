## Thumbnail analysis (State 10)
- **Title:** *A Leopard Hunts a Gorilla Family… Then the Silverback Wakes Up*
- **What the thumbnail must do:** show the silverback's fury and the leopard in one glance. The title supplies the story (a family, a baby, the hunt), so the image only has to deliver the clash.
- **Proven triggers used:**
  - **A huge, famous face:** a roaring silverback is one of the most clickable animal images there is.
  - **Predator vs protector:** a leopard everyone knows as a killer, facing something bigger.
  - **Consequence:** "Then the Silverback Wakes Up" promises the leopard made a mistake. The thumbnail shows the moment it realises.
- **Readability at phone size:**
  - the silverback's face fills about half the frame: open mouth, white canines, dark eyes,
  - the leopard's golden rosetted coat is the only bright warm shape against him,
  - golden mist behind gives a clean, bright background, with no busy leaves in front of faces.
- **Payoff in the video:** the main thumbnail is beat 1, the very first shot.
