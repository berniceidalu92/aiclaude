# THE OCTOPUS — Visual Analysis (State 7)

The final year in the life of a giant Pacific octopus in the cold kelp forests of British Columbia, from hunter and escape artist to devoted mother. It keeps the Nature's Coliseum real-documentary look, moved underwater for the first time.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** photorealistic cinematic underwater wildlife documentary, premium nature-film look, 16:9 widescreen. It should look like real footage filmed by divers and remote underwater cameras, never CGI or cartoon.
- **Palette:**
  - **The water:** cold green-blue, with golden-brown kelp, grey and ochre rock, white anemones and orange-purple sea stars.
  - **Ruby:** rich red-orange when calm, shifting to mottled grey, white or dark when camouflaged or alarmed, then fading to pale patchy grey as she starves.
  - **The eggs:** creamy white.
  - **Grade:** natural, slightly cool, never oversaturated.
- **Lighting:**
  - **Day:** shafts of sunlight slanting down through the kelp canopy, with floating particles catching the light.
  - **Night:** dim blue-black water lit only by a soft dive-light glow.
  - **The den:** deep shadow, with a cool rim of light from the entrance.
- **Camera:**
  - wide underwater lens for the kelp forest,
  - macro lens for skin, suckers, colour cells, eyes and eggs,
  - steady, slow, drifting moves like a diver hovering,
  - true high-frame-rate slow motion for the ink burst, the jet escape and the hatching.
- **Composition:** Ruby often blends into the rock until she moves. In the den scenes, the egg strings hang from the ceiling with Ruby wrapped beneath them.
- **Detail:** ultra-fine detail on skin texture, papillae, chromatophores, suckers, the eye's horizontal pupil, floating particles and the tiny embryos inside the eggs.
- **Mood:** wonder and mystery, tension in the hunt and the escape, and deep tenderness and sadness in the brooding and hatching.

## Shot range (mix it to suit the moment)
- **Long range:** the kelp forest, sea lions passing overhead, Ruby small on the seafloor, hatchlings drifting up toward the light.
- **Mid range:** Ruby hunting, flowing across the bottom, the sea lion attack, the male approaching, Ruby in the den with her eggs.
- **Close range:** eyes, suckers, skin changing colour and texture, the beak, ink, the arm regrowing, eggs and embryos, her fading skin, the last breaths.

## Seasons and lighting
- **Autumn and winter (hunting, escape, intelligence):** cool green water, slanting light, kelp swaying in the swell.
- **Night (the hunt):** blue-black water with a soft glow.
- **Spring (the male, laying eggs):** brighter green water, plankton in the light.
- **The six months of brooding:** mostly inside the dim den, with light from the entrance slowly changing.
- **Hatching:** soft light from the den entrance, with hatchlings drifting out into blue water.
- **Ending:** moonlit water near the surface, with a single hatchling among glowing plankton.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **Ruby:** a giant Pacific octopus with soft reddish-orange skin covered in small wrinkles and papillae, a large rounded mantle, golden eyes with horizontal pupils, and eight long arms lined with pale suckers, spanning wider than a car.
- **Ruby, camouflaged:** the same octopus with mottled grey-brown skin, raised spiky bumps and weed-like frills, blending into the rock.
- **Ruby, brooding:** the same octopus, thinner, with pale patchy grey skin, cloudy eyes and slowly moving arms.
- **The male:** a large giant Pacific octopus with reddish-brown skin, one arm slightly longer with a groove along its underside.
- **The hatchlings:** tiny translucent octopus hatchlings, each smaller than a grain of rice, with dark eyes, tiny arms and flickering colour dots.
- **The Steller sea lion:** a huge brown Steller sea lion with a thick neck, small ears and long whiskers.
- **Supporting animals:** a red rock crab, a sunflower sea star, a wolf eel.

## Locations
1. **The kelp forest:** tall golden-brown giant kelp rising from a rocky seafloor toward the sunlit surface.
2. **The rocky seafloor:** boulders, crevices, anemones and sea stars, with patches of sand.
3. **The hunting den:** a crack in the rocks with a small pile of empty crab shells outside it.
4. **The brooding den:** a small rocky cave with a low ceiling, where long strings of white eggs hang down.
5. **Open water:** blue-green water above the reef, with plankton glittering in the light.
6. **Brief side shots:** a veined octopus carrying a coconut shell on sand in Indonesia, and a mimic octopus on sand.

## Rules
- Every prompt is standalone, with the full character description, location and style.
- No text, logos, music, people, divers or equipment in frame, boats or gore.
- Correct anatomy: an octopus has exactly eight arms. The sea lion has two front flippers. No extra animals or limbs.
- The death and the sea lion attack are shown gently, with no blood, no injuries in focus and no decay.
- Image and video prompts follow the Wild Ruaha style: script text, a full prompt, then camera, lighting, mood and action lines (video: camera movement, subject motion, environment motion, duration).
