She is the fastest animal on land, and the hungriest hunter on the Serengeti.

Meet Asha, a cheetah mother raising three cubs alone on the most dangerous plains in Africa. She can reach highway speed in three seconds and outrun anything alive. Yet hyenas and lions take her kills almost as fast as she can make them, and every stolen meal is one her cubs will never eat.

So how does the fastest hunter on Earth survive, when she cannot hold on to what she catches?

Follow Asha through perfect chases and bitter losses, to the moment a lioness walks toward the grass where her cubs lie hidden, and the only way to save them is to run straight at the danger.

🐆 In this documentary:
• Why a cheetah's greatest strength is also her greatest weakness
• How hyenas and lions steal kills from the fastest hunter alive
• A mother's daring decoy run to lead a lioness away from her den
• The survival strategy that keeps cheetah cubs alive on the open plains
• Why fewer than 7,000 cheetahs remain in the wild
