## Thumbnail analysis (State 10)
- **Title:** *Harpy Eagles Never Miss. Then They Met Her.*
- **What the thumbnail must do:** show the situation in half a second: a mother with a baby on her back, flying between trees, and a giant eagle with its talons open right behind her. The title says the eagle never misses; the image shows the miss about to happen.
- **What the title adds that the image doesn't:** "Never miss" sets up the eagle as unbeatable; "Then they met her" promises the mother wins. The image never shows how.
- **Proven triggers used:**
  - **A mother and baby in danger:** the baby must be clearly visible on her back.
  - **A frozen near-catch:** the gap between talons and monkey is the question viewers click to answer.
  - **A famous, scary hunter:** the harpy eagle's crest and huge talons are instantly striking.
- **Readability at phone size:**
  - a plain blue sky behind the action, so both animals stand out as sharp shapes,
  - the monkey's long limbs and tail stretched out make an unmistakable silhouette,
  - the eagle's wings fully spread, its yellow feet and black talons forward.
- **Payoff in the video:** the main thumbnail is beat 1, the very first shot.
