Image Prompt: A vast sheer granite wall rising out of a deep valley, and high on it a tiny ledge where a female Himalayan ibex with a light sandy-grey coat, short curved horns and a slim, agile build stands perfectly still, three hundred meters of air beneath her, in the Karakoram mountains of northern Pakistan. Long range: aerial view from high above, the animals small against the immense walls. Camera: Aerial shot from a quiet high-altitude drone, sweeping along the mountain at a respectful distance from the animals.
