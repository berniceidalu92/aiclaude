## Description (paste into YouTube)

"The sight of a feather in a peacock's tail, whenever I gaze at it, makes me sick." Charles Darwin, 1860.

The man who explained evolution could not explain the peacock. A bird dragging a train longer than its body, so bright it can be seen across a forest, in a land full of tigers. By Darwin's own rules, it should not exist.

Follow one peacock through one monsoon in Bandhavgarh, central India, to find the answer. A fight with spurs for a patch of earth. A dance with a sound too deep for us to hear. Colours that aren't really there. A peahen who decides everything. A call that's a lie. And a tigress who tests whether that famous train is really a death sentence.
