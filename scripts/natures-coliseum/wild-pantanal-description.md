Everything on this river fears the caiman. Except her.

In the Pantanal of Brazil, the largest tropical wetland on Earth, a jaguar mother hunts crocodiles. She leaps from the riverbank onto a caiman's back and kills it with a single bite to the skull. But every kill makes a sound, and a huge male jaguar has been listening. He is not after her caiman. He is after her cub.

So how does a cat survive by hunting the one animal everything else is afraid of?

🐆 In this documentary:
• Why jaguars bite the skull, and how they kill caiman in the water
• A family of giant otters mobbing a jaguar off her kill
• A lost cub facing a green anaconda alone at night
• A mother jaguar taking on a much larger male to save her cub
• The cub's first caiman leap, and a wildfire that changes everything
