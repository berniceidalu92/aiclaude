# WILD ALASKA — Visual Analysis (State 7)

Same locked Nature's Coliseum look as Wild Pantanal and Wild Sundarbans, adapted to a subarctic salmon river.

## Visual style profile (locked for all prompts)
- **Style:** Photorealistic cinematic wildlife documentary, premium nature-film look, 16:9 widescreen, AI-rendered to feel like high-end telephoto footage.
- **Palette:** Warm honey-gold light over rich chocolate-brown bear fur, deep spruce greens, slate-grey wet rock, white rushing water and flashes of crimson salmon. A soft warm golden grade with gently lifted blacks and moderate saturation.
- **Lighting:** Long, low Alaskan summer sun (golden light for hours), backlit rim light glowing on wet fur, sparkling spray and water droplets, soft mist rising off the river.
- **Camera:** Long telephoto lens (200–600mm feel), very shallow depth of field, compressed perspective. Camera low at water level or at the bears' eye level. Fast shutter freezing spray, leaping salmon and fights. Extreme close-ups for emotional beats.
- **Composition:** Subject in a narrow sharp focal plane, framed by blurred foreground spray, grass or spruce branches. Wide shots place bears low with the falls, river and mountains layered behind.
- **Detail:** Ultra-fine micro-detail in focus (wet fur, water droplets, claws, teeth, salmon scales) and creamy bokeh everywhere else.
- **Mood:** Intimate, powerful, majestic. Visceral in fights, without gore.

## Lighting exceptions (scene-specific)
- **Night / dawn on the river:** blue-grey pre-dawn light with mist on the water.
- **September:** red and gold autumn tundra, cooler light, first snow on the peaks.
- **Den ending:** soft falling snow, blue dusk with a warm glow on fur.

## Character locks (repeat word for word in every prompt)
- **Tula:** an adult female Alaskan brown bear (coastal grizzly), lean after winter, with a chocolate-brown coat, lighter golden-tipped fur on her shoulders, a broad dished face, small rounded ears and dark eyes. By September she is round and heavy with a thick shining coat.
- **Bo:** a five-month-old male brown bear cub, the bigger of two, with fluffy dark brown fur, a pale cream collar around his neck, round ears and bright curious eyes.
- **Fern:** a five-month-old female brown bear cub, smaller and lighter, with soft cinnamon-brown fur, a pale cream collar around her neck and timid eyes.
- **Scar:** a massive old male brown bear over 450 kilos, with a dark brown coat, a huge muscular shoulder hump, a broad heavy head and a pale scar across his muzzle.
- **Ridge:** a huge young male brown bear, nearly as big as Scar, with a lighter blond-brown coat, a thick neck, a powerful shoulder hump and no scars at first (fresh wounds on his neck after the first fight).
- **The wolf:** a lean grey wolf with a pale chest, long legs and amber eyes.
- **Salmon:** bright crimson sockeye salmon with green heads.

## Locations
1. **The falls:** a wide, low waterfall about two meters high, white water pouring over a rock ledge (the lip), salmon leaping up its face.
2. **The pool below the falls:** churning white water where bears sit chest-deep.
3. **The riffles downstream:** shallow fast water over round stones.
4. **The riverbank:** tall grass, spruce forest, a single tall spruce tree.
5. **The mountains:** volcanic peaks above lakes and tundra, the den slope.

## Rules
- Every prompt is standalone: full character description, location and style every time.
- No text, no logos, no music, no human presence (no viewing platforms, people or boats), no gore.
- Fights are frozen at the moment of contact (spray, claws, open jaws), with no blood focus.
